Quick Rules (click to expand)

Goal: Reduce your opponent's blood to 0 or below. If both reach 0 at once, it's a draw.

Distance: Both players share one distance track (0 clinch – 4 far), starting at 2. Techniques must be used within their valid distance, or they miss (+1 flaw).

Qi: Max 5, starts at 5, and recovers 1 at the start of each turn. Playing a card costs the qi shown on it.

Flaw: Each stack lowers your technique speed by 1; 1 stack is removed at the start of your turn. Internal Injury: Each stack increases damage taken by 1. Poison: Take damage equal to your stacks at the start of your turn, then decay by 1.

Qi Return: Played techniques go to the qi-return pile; when it reaches 4 cards (5 with DLC2), all return to your hand.

Showdown: Reveal simultaneously and compare speed (technique speed − flaw + taiji stance − slow). The higher acts first, the lower acts second; on a tie, both act second and damage is −1 (min 1).

Ultimate: Once per game; removed from play after use.
